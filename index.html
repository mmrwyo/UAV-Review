Reviewer guidebook and submission process

Your first assignment

Ali Torabi (Reviewer A) and Md Omor Faruk (Reviewer B) independently evaluate the same 100 title/abstract records. Abiam Asif Khalid adjudicates after both submit. Other coauthors may help retrieve papers and check extraction after assignment; they should not complete either reviewer's form.

  1. Select your name and open your workspace. Read the rules below.
  2. Read each title and abstract. Choose Include, Exclude or Uncertain. Include and Uncertain both advance to full-text assessment.
  3. Give your own reason and a supporting phrase or locator. Record time and any external help. Do not view the other reviewer's or AI decisions before submitting.
  4. Use “Download draft JSON” at the end of each session. Reopen it using “Resume your own saved JSON.”
  5. When all 100 records are complete, tick the declaration and download the completed JSON and CSV. Keep both files.
  6. Open your named Drive folder and upload both. The page cannot upload automatically. Notify the coordinator of your filenames and any unresolved questions.

Shared Drive folders are not access-isolated. Independence is a team procedure. Do not open another reviewer's submitted files until both independent passes are complete. If you previously saw AI recommendations, disclose that exposure; do not claim the review was blinded.

Eligibility rules

A: direct crop UAV evidence with a state/decision link, or foundation-model perception as enabling evidence. B: supporting crop methods with a foundation-model, twin or agent mechanism linked to state updating or decisions; record the absent UAV link. C: transferable aerial robotics with a specific state, planning, action or feedback function. R: relevant reviews for overlap/citation discovery, separately from primary studies.

Include conventional adaptive monitoring and baselines. Ordinary static classification with no eligible mechanism or state/decision link is background, not primary mapped evidence. Do not require all three mechanisms, field experiments, a comparator or positive findings. Conceptual papers require a specified pathway. Preprints are eligible separately; link published versions. Rank, citation count and the 160-reference target are not inclusion rules.

Dates: 1 January 2015 through the actual search date (frozen calibration source: 26–27 October 2026). Ambiguous online dates need checking. Missing abstracts, unavailable full texts and unassessable language are uncertainty/access issues, not automatic scientific exclusions.

Exclusion codes, first applicable: E1 outside date window; E2 ineligible source type; E3 outside eligible crop/aerial context; E4 no eligible mechanism/state/decision link; E5 insufficient methods/architecture detail (normally full-text only; not for a missing abstract).

Foundation models are broadly pretrained, adaptable models; ordinary deep learning is not automatically a foundation model. Agentic systems use state/observations to choose action or tool sequences with feedback; a chatbot alone is insufficient. For twins, record physical referent, state updates, synchronization and action coupling. Automatic two-way flow is assessed, not a universal eligibility gate. Preserve author labels separately from your classification.

How to fill each field

Rationale: one or two sentences explaining your decision. Evidence: quote a short relevant phrase or identify the title/abstract paragraph. Route: A, B, C, R or Unclear. Timing: use the optional timer or enter measured active seconds; pause for interruptions. If not measured, explain in notes. AI assistance: enter “None” or identify the tool, task and prior exposure. Do not copy an AI decision as your independent judgment.

Example only, not a sample answer: “Uncertain; abstract missing and title does not establish the state-to-decision pathway; evidence: metadata only; route Unclear.” Never infer absent methods or invent outcomes.

Abiam's adjudication

Import completed JSON files from A and B only after both have submitted. The interface flags decision differences and Uncertain records. Read the source and both rationales; discuss discrepancies, then record your final decision and reason. Preserve A/B originals. You may inspect any record, including agreement cases. Record rule changes and which records need reassessment. An unresolved access issue remains Uncertain. The coordinator calculates agreement and screening time from the initial files, not adjudicated decisions.

Later full-text and extraction work

This version covers the 100-record title/abstract calibration and adjudication only. It does not replace later full-text forms. After calibration, independently assess the planned five full texts. Record exact PDF version and page/table locators, eligibility reason, route, mechanism role, feedback, comparator, validation fields/seasons, leakage concerns, outcomes, uncertainty, latency/resources and human oversight. Separate prediction, mission and agricultural outcomes. Use NR for not reported; missing is not zero. Keep field, simulation, offline and conceptual evidence distinct. No summed quality score or automatic quality cutoff.

File naming and long-term reuse

Downloads use UAV_CAL100_[name]_[stage]_[DRAFT-or-COMPLETED]_[UTC-timestamp].json or .csv. The timestamp is the version. Never overwrite an earlier submitted original. A correction gets a new timestamp and a note explaining changed record IDs. JSON is the authoritative machine-readable backup; CSV is for inspection and analysis. Keep sample IDs and record IDs unchanged.

Upload reviewer files to the named reviewer folders, adjudication to Abiam's folder, and later full-text/extraction forms to “04_Full_Text_and_Extraction_Returns.” The coordinator checks IDs, completeness and versions, then copies accepted files to “05_Accepted_and_Locked”; originals remain unchanged. “Locked” is a workflow label, not a permission restriction. The coordinator updates the manifest with filename, reviewer, stage, date, source sample, checksum and status before reuse.

Use “06_AI_Assisted_Separate” for AI outputs. Human decisions are never filled automatically. Do not put responses, private data packs or licensed PDFs on public GitHub.
